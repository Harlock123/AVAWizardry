# Credits: ambience

The ambience loops AVAGard plays quietly under the music in the dungeon (copied from the author's AVABand): shallow for levels 1-19, deep for 20-39, abyss for 40-50. All were released under CC0 on OpenGameArt.org.

## ambient-dungeon-abyss.ogg

- **Droning Sound Effects (drone60.wav)** — bretbernhoft, 2024-11-20 (OpenGameArt upload date)
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/droning-sound-effects
- Licence: CC0 (OpenGameArt 'License(s): CC0' field on the asset page)
- Notes: 120 s dense low drone; converted from 32-bit WAV to Vorbis q3. Fairly loud (-10 dB mean) - lower the gain in-game. Page states files are public domain / no attribution; loop seam not verified.

## ambient-dungeon-deep.ogg

- **underwater or space engine rumble (deep_rumble.ogg)** — gmason, 2024-09-04 (OpenGameArt upload date)
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/underwater-or-space-engine-rumble
- Licence: CC0 (OpenGameArt 'License(s): CC0' field on the asset page)
