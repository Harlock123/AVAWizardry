# How to play

The mad archmage Morthrain has stolen the Amulet of Thal from the Overlord Kessar and fled to the bottom of the Proving Grounds, a ten-level maze beneath the castle. Kessar will make champions of any party that brings it back.

You play a party of up to six adventurers. They are made at the Training Grounds (Edge of Town), gathered at the tavern, rested at the inn, equipped at the trading post and, when things go badly, raised at the temple.

## Getting started

- **Quickest:** choose *New game with a ready-made party* on the title screen. Six characters (two fighters, a priest, a thief, a mage and a bishop) are made and armed for you, and they're already in the party.
- **Your own party:** choose *New game: make your own characters*, then go to Edge of Town > Training Grounds > Create. Make up to twenty characters, then add six of them to the party at the tavern.
- Go to Edge of Town and enter the maze. Fight on the first level until your characters have earned enough experience, then rest at the inn: that's where levels are gained.

## Things worth knowing

- Experience only becomes a new level at the inn. Even the free stables will do.
- The first three members of the party are the front rank. Only they can fight hand to hand, and only they are hit in hand-to-hand combat. Put fighters in front and spellcasters behind.
- Every spell level has its own number of casts, restored by rest.
- Death isn't the end: the temple can raise the dead, for a tithe. Raising can fail, leaving ashes; raising ashes can fail too, and then the character is lost for good.
- The game saves itself whenever the party enters the castle, rests, makes camp or quits.
- Good and evil characters won't travel together. Neutral characters go with either.
