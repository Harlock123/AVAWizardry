# The maze

Ten levels of 20 by 20 squares, the same in every game. You see the corridor ahead in wireframe, as in 1981. (0, 0) is the south-west corner of each level, and the stairs from the castle come down there.

## Moving

- Up arrow or W: forward. Left/right arrows or A/D: turn. Down arrow or X: turn around.
- C: make camp (inspect, equip, cast healing spells, reorder, or save and quit).
- S: search for a fight (to earn experience without walking).

Doors are drawn in amber. Walking into a wall says OUCH!. Some walls are secret doors you can walk through. With light (LUMA or LUMAR) you see further, and you see the secret doors.

## Hazards

- **Darkness:** nothing can be seen, even with light.
- **Spinners** turn the party to face a random way, without a word. Watch the compass.
- **Teleporters** move the party somewhere else on the level.
- **Pits** wound whoever falls in. **Chutes** drop the party to the level below.
- **Dead air:** spells cast there fizzle.
- **Rooms** have guarded chests, once each trip.

## Getting about

The stairs down are always far from the stairs up. Lifts serve levels 1 to 4 and, for parties carrying the Warden's Ribbon, levels 4 to 9. The ribbon is guarded on the fourth level by the Iron Warden; it also opens the sealed stair down from that level.

LOKAR shows where the party is. REDIRA (or a Scroll of Return) takes the party home from anywhere, but its gold stays behind. PASSAR moves the party to any square you name, and naming solid rock is fatal.

## The automap

A convenience the original didn't have: the map beside the text shows every square the party has seen on this level. You can turn it off in Settings if you prefer graph paper.
