# Keys

Every choice on screen shows its key. Click the buttons, or press the keys.

## Everywhere

- Letters and digits: the choices shown.
- Enter or Space: continue, or confirm typed text.
- Esc: back.
- F1: help. F2: settings (the game waits for you).

## In the maze

- Up arrow or W: forward.
- Left arrow or A, Right arrow or D: turn.
- Down arrow or X: turn around.
- C: camp. S: look for a fight.

## On the title screen

- C: continue. R: new game with a ready-made party. N: new game, making your own. S: settings. H: help. Q: quit.
