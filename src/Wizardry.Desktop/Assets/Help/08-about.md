# About

AVAWizardry is an independent, unofficial game in the style of Wizardry: Proving Grounds of the Mad Overlord (Sir-Tech, 1981). Its rules follow the original's design: the races and classes and their requirements, alignment, the experience table, spell levels and casts, the front rank, chests and traps, the temple, aging, class changes. Its maze, story, names, spell names, monsters, items, pictures, text, music and code are its own. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the owners of the Wizardry name.
