# What's new

## 2026-10-12

- Settings > Audio buffer has a new Extra large choice (4096 frames, about 85 ms), and Automatic now uses it in a virtual machine, where the sound could still be choppy at Large.

- First release. All ten levels of the Proving Grounds, the castle (tavern, inn, trading post, temple, training grounds), eight classes and five races, 47 spells, 50 monsters, 76 items, traps and chests, the Iron Warden and Morthrain.
- Wireframe first-person maze, an optional automap, and vector pictures of every monster family and castle building.
- A ready-made party for a quick start, rescue patrols (or the classic rule), lifts between levels, and sound and music.
