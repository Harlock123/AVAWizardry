# The castle

The castle's market street is the menu between trips into the maze.

## The Gilded Flagon (tavern)

Add characters to the party (up to six), remove them, inspect them (equipment, spells, trading) and reorder the party. *Divvy gold* shares the party's gold out evenly.

## The Wayfarer's Rest (inn)

Resting restores every spell, heals, and turns experience into new levels. Each week costs money and adds to a character's age:

- Stables: free, but no healing.
- Cot: 10 GP a week, 2 HP.
- Economy room: 50 GP a week, 6 HP.
- Merchant suite: 200 GP a week, 15 HP.
- Royal suite: 500 GP a week, 30 HP.

A character stays until fully healed or out of money. At each new level every attribute may rise or fall; the older the character, the likelier it falls. A character too old, or whose vitality falls too low, dies of old age.

## Harrow's Trading Post

Buy arms, armour, potions and scrolls; sell anything (for half its price); identify an unknown item; or lift a curse. The basic stock never runs out. Other items are few, and what you sell goes on the shelves. *Pool gold* puts the whole party's gold in one purse.

## Temple of Saint Aldric

The priests cure paralysis and stoning, and raise the dead and even ashes, for a tithe of 100, 200, 250 or 500 GP per level of the patient. A dead character's own purse can pay.

## Edge of Town

Enter the maze, visit the Training Grounds, or leave the game (it is saved).

## Training Grounds

Create, inspect, rename and delete characters, and change class. A new class starts over at level 1, with attributes back to the race's base, and is a few years older, but it keeps its spells, items and gold. That's how lords, samurai and ninjas are usually made.
