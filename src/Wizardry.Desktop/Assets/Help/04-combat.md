# Combat

Monsters come in up to four groups. Until a group is identified, you see only what it looks like ("Man in Robes"). Higher-level parties identify foes more often, and NOMEN names them all.

Each round, every member who can act gives an order:

- **Fight:** front-rank members only, against the first two groups. Fighters, samurai, lords and ninjas swing more often as they rise in level.
- **Parry:** improves the member's armour class for the round.
- **Spell:** choose a spell, then its target.
- **Use:** an item that casts a spell (potions, scrolls, some weapons and rings).
- **Dispel:** priests, bishops and lords can banish undead.
- **Hide:** thieves and ninjas can slip into the shadows, then strike from hiding at any group, for double damage.
- **Run:** the whole party tries to flee. You can't run from a guardian's lair.

Then everyone acts in order of a roll, helped by agility.

## Armour class

Armour class starts at 10 and goes down as armour is worn: lower is better. Below 0 is excellent.

## Trouble

- **Poison** drains hit points as you walk until it wears off, or until PURGA, an antidote or the castle cures it.
- **Paralysis** and **stoning** take a member out of the fight. SOLVA cures paralysis; the temple cures both.
- **Energy drain** (wights, wraiths, vampires) costs a level.
- Some foes can deal a mortal blow. Some breathe fire, frost or poison on the whole party.

## After the fight

Experience and gold are shared among the survivors. Sometimes there's a chest. A thief can inspect it (others can, with less skill), and anyone can try to disarm the trap they name. Name the wrong trap and it goes off. SPEKIA names the trap almost every time. Or just open it and hope.

Items from the maze are unidentified (?SWORD). The trading post identifies them for half their price, and bishops can try for free (careful: a cursed item may stick to the bishop).
