# Races and classes

## Races

```
RACE    STR  IQ PIE VIT AGI LUC
HUMAN     8   8   5   8   8   9
ELF       7  10  10   6   9   6
DWARF    10   7  10  10   5   6
GNOME     7   7  10   8  10   7
HOBBIT    5   7   7   6  10  15
```

At creation you spread a handful of bonus points (usually 5 to 9; now and then many more) over these. A low roll can be rerolled.

## Classes

- **Fighter** (STR 11): the best hit points and the most swings. Any alignment.
- **Mage** (IQ 11): mage spells. Few hit points. Any alignment.
- **Priest** (PIE 11): priest spells and dispelling the undead. Good or evil.
- **Thief** (AGI 11): inspects and disarms chests, hides. Neutral or evil.
- **Bishop** (IQ 12, PIE 12): learns both kinds of spells, slowly, and identifies items. Good or evil.
- **Samurai** (STR 15, IQ 11, PIE 10, VIT 14, AGI 10): a fighter who learns mage spells from level 4. Good or neutral.
- **Lord** (STR 15, IQ 12, PIE 12, VIT 15, AGI 14, LUC 15): a fighter who learns priest spells from level 4. Good only.
- **Ninja** (17 in everything): fights best barehanded and unarmoured, with a chance of a killing blow. Evil only.

Samurai, lords and ninjas are hard to make at creation. Usually a character reaches them by changing class at the Training Grounds once their attributes have grown.

## Alignment

Good and evil characters won't share a party. Some classes demand an alignment, and the Sword of the Warden and Lords' Garb refuse anyone who isn't good.
